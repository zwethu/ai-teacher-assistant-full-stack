For backend

!! IMPORTANT: do NOT pass --env-vars-file env.yaml !!
env.yaml only has 4 variables, but the service needs ~20 (FIREBASE_RTDB_URL,
AGENT_ENGINE_RESOURCE_NAME, CLOUD_TASKS_*, ...). --env-vars-file REPLACES the
whole set, so deploying with it wipes the other 16 vars. That broke production
on 2026-08-12: the backend could not write agentRuns to RTDB and every AI
draft failed with "Lost the live connection". The vars were restored from
revision 00016.

Env vars persist across deploys on their own, so just deploy the code:

gcloud run services update ai-teacher-backend --region asia-southeast1 --update-env-vars KEY=VALUE
